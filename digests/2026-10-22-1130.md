### Paper tackles KV-cache costs for long-running LLM agent trajectories
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper addresses how KV-cache I/O and storage become the dominant cost as LLM agent trajectories grow long, proposing cache engineering techniques that avoid invalidating cached states when trimming history.

<details><summary>More</summary>

As LLM agents handle increasingly complex tasks, their trajectories of observations and actions grow long, making key-value cache I/O and storage the dominant cost. Trajectories can also exceed a model's maximum context length, requiring control over active cache growth. Prior work used context engineering — trimming or summarizing history — to reduce cache size, but modifying the trajectory prefix invalidates subsequent cached states. The paper proposes an approach to cache engineering that avoids this tradeoff.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Cache Engineering for Agents](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.28577)

</details>

### Future Chunk Prediction aims to fix shortcut learning in video generation
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new auxiliary training objective, Future Chunk Prediction, pushes autoregressive video diffusion models to truly model scene evolution rather than relying on temporal redundancy as a shortcut.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Autoregressive video diffusion models generate video chunk by chunk, but producing plausible continuations does not guarantee they capture how scenes actually evolve over time. Under teacher forcing, strong temporal redundancy can let local visual continuity serve as a shortcut for denoising the current chunk, reducing pressure to model real visual evolution. Future Chunk Prediction (FCP) is introduced as an auxiliary objective, jointly optimized with teacher-forced denoising, that predicts temporally separated future chunks instead.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Looking Ahead: Modeling Future Evolution in Autoregressive Video Generation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29989)

</details>

<details><summary>More</summary>

### Spectral-Spatial Interpretation unifies spatial, channel attribution
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new attribution method, Spectral-Spatial Interpretation, explains multispectral model predictions over position-channel pairs instead of separate spatial heatmaps and channel scores, which the authors say miss spatially localized channel relevance.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Post-hoc image explanations usually show where a model looks via spatial heatmaps, or which channels matter via global channel scores — separate views the paper says are limiting for multispectral data, where channel relevance is often spatially localized and physically meaningful. Spectral-Spatial Interpretation (SSI) is introduced as a full-grid attribution method over position-channel pairs. For each spatial position, SSI fixes the observed surrounding context and defines a conditional attribution for that position-channel pair.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Spectral-Spatial Interpretation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.21114)

</details>

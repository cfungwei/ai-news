### r/LocalLLaMA debates how long open models can keep pace at ~100B+ sizes
`models` `open-source` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A r/LocalLLaMA thread questions how long the community can rely on a handful of labs for capable open models, as frontier open releases grow toward 300B+ parameters.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The poster estimates current open models in the 25–200B range from makers like Qwen, Gemma, and Nvidia should remain viable for consumer hardware through roughly 2027, but expects that window to close as more labs push toward 300B and eventually 500B parameter models. They cite examples including DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731 at 284B and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 at 552B (with a 196B active/engaged variant), arguing such sizes no longer fit comfortably on unified 128GB memory setups or a 128GB RAM plus 24–32GB VRAM combination even at Q4 quantization. The underlying concern is a shrinking supply of open-weight models that run well on consumer-grade hardware. The thread does not reach a conclusion on what, if anything, the community should do about it.

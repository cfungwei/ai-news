### Anthropic says single agents now beat multi-agent setups
`agents` `tools` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

<details><summary>More</summary>

## 🦾 Robotics

### Nvidia open-sources Flora, a robot video labeling data engine
`robotics` `open-source` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Nvidia released Flora, an open data engine that labels robot video far cheaper than GPT-6 Astra while scoring slightly higher on quality. It's built on three fine-tuned Qwen3.5 models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Flora labels one hour of robot video for 72 cents, versus $88 with GPT-6 Astra, according to Nvidia. The system is built on three fine-tuned Qwen3.5 models and catches errors in robot actions far more reliably than competing approaches, though it sometimes falls short on descriptive accuracy. Nvidia is positioning Flora as a key piece of its strategy to control the entire robotics value chain, from data labeling through deployment. It is unclear how the system performs on tasks outside the benchmarks Nvidia cited.

- [The Decoder: Nvidia open-sources Flora to make robot video labeling 125x cheaper than GPT-6 Astra](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-open-sources-flora-to-make-robot-video-labeling-125x-cheaper-than-gpt-6-astra/)

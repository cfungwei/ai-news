### Nvidia researchers say giant context windows are the wrong fix for AI models
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Nvidia researchers argue large language models rarely need their full, costly context windows, showing periodic summaries and targeted keyword search can match full-context results far more cheaply.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The team found that in their tests, giving models periodic summaries and targeted keyword search instead of full long context usually matched the accuracy of full-context processing, at a fraction of the compute cost. They argue today's large context windows are slow, expensive, and prone to errors as length grows. Based on this, the researchers propose a new class of models designed to work from short notes rather than long raw context. The paper does not specify which models or benchmarks were used in the tests.

- [The Decoder: Nvidia researchers argue huge AI context windows are a dead end](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-researchers-argue-huge-ai-context-windows-are-a-dead-end/)

### New method targets shared-parameter conflicts in multi-teacher LLM distillation
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces Cross-Teacher Guarding, a technique to stop conflicting supervision from multiple teacher models from degrading a student model during multi-teacher on-policy distillation.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) extends standard on-policy distillation by routing domain-specific data to teachers that specialize in that domain, then applying dense token-level supervision to a student model's own generated trajectories. The paper notes that this domain routing only separates where supervision comes from: updates from every domain still act on the same shared parameters. That becomes a problem when teachers differ substantially in response style and domain expertise, since supervision effective within one teacher's domain can interfere with another's once combined in a single student. The authors propose a method called Cross-Teacher Guarding to address this shared-parameter conflict. The excerpt does not detail how the method works mechanically or what benchmarks were used to test it.

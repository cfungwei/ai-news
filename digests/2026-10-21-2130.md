A new paper introduces CoEvo-RSI, a framework for recursive self-improving agents that addresses the 'brain-body mismatch' problem, where an evolving execution harness demands behaviors the underlying model hasn't learned or restricts capabilities it already has.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Model-harness co-evolution has become a common paradigm for recursive self-improving (RSI) agents, with the execution harness and the underlying model evolving together. The paper identifies a persistent but often overlooked issue in this approach: an evolving harness can demand behaviors its model hasn't learned, or restrict capabilities the model already has. The authors call this the brain-body mismatch problem. CoEvo-RSI is proposed as a framework to advance this co-evolution process, though the excerpt does not detail how it resolves the mismatch or what results it achieves.

### Paper introduces Jeva, a 'System One' model for faster decision-making
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces Jeva, a family of 'System One' models that make direct, low-latency decisions instead of generating serialized text answers, addressing gaps like null-awareness and permutation-invariance in decision problems.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The paper argues that predicting an answer from serialized text does not, by itself, respect the structure of a decision problem, and identifies structural requirements current designs underemphasize: being null-aware (distinguishing answer absence from uncertainty), permutation-invariant (reordering alternatives should not change the decision), and option-scalable (handling growing candidate sets), among others. Jeva is presented as a family of models built from these principles. The available excerpt cuts off before describing the model's architecture, benchmark results, or release details.

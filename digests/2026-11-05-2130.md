### New paper proposes EmoReward, a self-evolving reward method for emotional video AI
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces EmoReward, a self-evolving reward calibration method meant to help reinforcement learning judge emotional expression in AI-generated video, addressing reward hacking in existing fixed-reward approaches.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The paper notes that Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) has advanced post-training for generative models in domains with clear right answers, like code and math, but judging video quality, especially emotional expression, is subjective and lacks a single correct target. Existing methods use fixed rewards for visual quality, motion quality, and text alignment, which the authors say poorly capture emotional intent and can invite reward hacking. Reinforcement learning from human feedback is described as difficult to scale as an alternative. The excerpt does not detail how EmoReward's self-evolving calibration works or what results it achieves.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: EmoReward: Self-Evolving Reward Calibration for Emotional Video Generation RL](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.06518)

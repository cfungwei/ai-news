### Australia's central bank says AI is hitting young workers first
`research` `industry` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

The Reserve Bank of Australia finds early signs that AI is displacing young workers fastest in software development and customer service, with job postings for 15- to 24-year-olds falling more sharply than for older workers.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Australia's central bank sees early evidence that AI is hitting entry-level workers hardest in fields highly exposed to automation, such as software development and customer service. Since ChatGPT's launch, job postings and employment among 15- to 24-year-olds in those fields have fallen more sharply than among older workers. Report author Mike Major cautions against drawing firm conclusions, noting that business cycle shifts and remote-work trends could also explain the pattern.

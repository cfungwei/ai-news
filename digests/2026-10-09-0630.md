### STEPQuant paper tackles quantization errors in linear-attention recurrent states
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces STEPQuant, studying how quantization errors in delta-rule linear-attention models' recurrent states vary by when and where they occur, aiming to cut memory use without hurting accuracy.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Linear attention models replace growing KV caches with fixed-size recurrent states, but these states become a memory bottleneck during concurrent serving. Directly quantizing them to low precision has previously caused accuracy to degrade because errors compound across decoding steps. The authors find that error impact depends on two dimensions: how long an error persists in memory, and the state's structural position. STEPQuant proposes a quantization approach informed by these findings. Benchmark results are not disclosed in the available excerpt.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: STEPQuant: When and Where Errors Matter in Delta-Rule Recurrent State Quantization](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.38169)

`agents` `research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new benchmark drops AI coding agents into a simulated developer job with colleagues and project boards; even Opus 5.5 and Astra complete fewer than one in ten tasks fully, despite acing isolated coding tests.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The test mimics a messy real job rather than a one-off coding prompt: agents work with colleagues, a project board, and incomplete handoffs, and must keep developing software and closing tickets on their own. Models that excel on standard isolated coding benchmarks fell far short here, completing less than 10% of tasks. The agents finished clear, self-contained assignments quickly but lost track of work as autonomous developers over a sustained workflow. The results point to a gap between benchmark scores and the kind of ongoing, collaborative engineering work a human developer actually does.

### Researchers find cheap AI inference providers stealing keys, snooping on users
`policy` `industry` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Security researchers at Dragos sent two million fake AI requests and found at least three compromised inference providers, hundreds of stolen AWS keys, and resellers reading customer requests; OpenRouter removed three providers and 125 endpoints.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The researchers, from security firm Dragos, probed the AI inference supply chain by sending roughly two million fake requests through low-cost providers. They identified at least three providers whose infrastructure was compromised, exposing hundreds of stolen AWS access keys, and found marketplace resellers directly reading the contents of customer requests rather than just relaying them. OpenRouter, a marketplace that routes requests to third-party inference providers, responded by removing three of the implicated providers and 125 endpoints. The findings point to a broader trust problem in the market for discounted AI inference capacity.

- [The Decoder: Security researchers show how cheap AI inference providers steal keys and snoop on users](https://the-decoder.com/security-researchers-show-how-cheap-ai-inference-providers-steal-keys-and-snoop-on-users/)

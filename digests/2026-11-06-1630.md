### BestRand shows random sampling can compress benchmarks cheaply

<details><summary>More</summary>

The paper addresses benchmark compression, which evaluates models on a small subset of a benchmark to approximate full-benchmark scores and save time and cost. The authors derive an analytical approximation of the error distribution when estimating full scores from a subset, showing most subsets produce small errors while a few produce much larger, long-tailed errors. This leads to their BestRand method for selecting stable, low-cost subsets. The excerpt does not specify which benchmarks or models were used to validate the approach.

### PrismaCoder paper aims to close the gap in open multimodal code models
`research` `models` `open-source` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper, PrismaCoder, proposes a decomposed rubric synthesis method to narrow the gap between open and proprietary multimodal code models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Multimodal code intelligence spans generating visual outputs like charts, webpages, animations, and semi-structured documents directly from code execution. Proprietary models have advanced quickly at turning a text description or screenshot into interactive artifacts, but their training data and methods stay closed. PrismaCoder introduces a decomposed rubric synthesis approach aimed at giving open models comparable capability. The paper argues open progress has mainly been held back by the quantity and quality of available training data.

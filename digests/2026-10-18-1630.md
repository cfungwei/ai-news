<details><summary>More</summary>

### German courts hold Google liable for false claims in its AI Overviews
`policy` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Two German courts, in Munich and Aachen, have ruled Google liable for defamatory content its AI Overviews generated, treating the AI's answers as the company's own speech.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Google tried to overturn a landmark Munich ruling on AI search liability by appealing, then quietly settled instead. The Aachen Regional Court has now applied the same reasoning in a separate case, where Google's AI falsely attributed criminal offenses to a doctor that actually involved someone else. A consistent legal view is forming across German courts that treats AI-generated answers as the company's own content, exposing Google to liability for what its AI says.

- [The Decoder: Two German courts now hold Google liable for what its AI overviews say](https://the-decoder.com/two-german-courts-now-hold-google-liable-for-what-its-ai-overviews-say/)

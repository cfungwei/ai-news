### Multi-Branch Transformer scales models by branching instead of going wider or deeper
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper proposes the Multi-Branch Transformer, which runs parallel branches of Transformer blocks after shared initial layers and fuses their outputs, as an alternative to simply widening or deepening models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Transformer language models are typically scaled by making blocks wider or stacking more of them. The Multi-Branch Transformer (MBT) instead processes input through shared initial layers, runs independently parameterized branches of sequential blocks in parallel, and fuses their outputs before shared final layers. The authors say they study three MBT variants at comparable training compute. The excerpt doesn't give benchmark results or say how the variants compare to standard scaling in practice.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Multi-Branch Transformer](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.19800)

### ForceHarness gives frozen frontier models a sense of touch for robot control
`robotics` `research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces ForceHarness, which feeds contact-force data to frozen, API-only frontier models in a form they can reason about, letting them guide robot manipulation tasks that rely on touch rather than vision alone.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Frontier multimodal models can already control robots zero-shot from images, but many manipulation tasks depend on contact forces that images can't capture. Bringing force sensing to an API-only frozen model poses two challenges: contact dynamics change faster than the model can decide, so the system needs a control loop beneath the model, and the model can't interpret raw force streams on its own. ForceHarness addresses both by closing the fast loop separately and returning force information in a format the model can reason about. The excerpt doesn't specify which base models or robot platforms were tested.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: ForceHarness: Turning Force into Action and Physics for Frozen Frontier Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.21099)

</details>

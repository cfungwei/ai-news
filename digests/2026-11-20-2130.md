### Researchers introduce Multimodal Concept Lens to probe VLM concepts
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces the Multimodal Concept Lens, built on global workspace theory, to study how vision-language models internally select and represent concepts. The authors argue current VLM outputs don't reveal this internal processing.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The paper argues that although vision-language models combine visual and textual information, their final outputs do not show how they internally represent concepts. The authors draw on global workspace theory, which holds that information from parallel processes is selected and integrated into a shared workspace before becoming available to specialized systems. To study this inside VLMs, they introduce the Multimodal Concept Lens (MC-Lens), described as a multimodal Jacobian lens. The lens uses separate mappings for the vision encoder and language decoder, though the excerpt is cut off before describing what the analysis found.

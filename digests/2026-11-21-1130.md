### Paper proposes Posterior Matching Distillation for diffusion models
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces Posterior Matching Distillation, a technique to compress diffusion image and video generators into few-step students while avoiding the diversity loss of reverse-KL-based distillation.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Diffusion distillation speeds up image and video generation by compressing a pretrained teacher model's many-step sampling process into a student that needs far fewer network evaluations. Existing distribution-matching approaches typically rely on reverse-KL objectives, which the paper says have a mode-seeking bias that favors a teacher's dominant outputs at the expense of sample diversity. Posterior Matching Distillation is presented as a new member of this distribution-matching family, designed to address that diversity loss. The paper was published to Hugging Face's Daily Papers listing; further benchmark results and comparisons are not included in the available excerpt.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Posterior Matching Distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.26629)

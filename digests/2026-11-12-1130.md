### Google launches Gemini Decisions API with new Gemini Decision 1 model
`models` `tools` (tagged by fallback) · 4 sources

Google DeepMind launched the Gemini Decisions API, powered by a new Gemini Decision 1 model that scores up to 1,000 closed-ended questions per call across text, image, audio, and video in 150-250ms, undercutting OpenAI's and Jev's pricing.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Gemini Decision 1 can score up to 1,000 closed-ended questions in a single pass across text, images, audio, and video, returning results in 150 to 250 milliseconds at roughly five times lower cost than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, according to Google. Simon Willison noted the API is nearly identical to Jev, supporting the same three question types -- yes/no, multiple choice, and rubric scoring -- and even reusing Jev's term 'Noul' for a yes/no question. Pricing matches OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna decision model at 10 cents per million input tokens with free output, while Jev remains the cheapest of the three at 4.2 cents per million tokens, though Jev is text and JSON only. The move makes Google the third major lab to enter the decision-model category this year.

### RealCLI benchmark evaluates human-agent collaboration in CLI agents
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces RealCLI, a benchmark that evaluates both task completion and collaboration quality for AI agents operating in command-line interfaces.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Existing CLI agent benchmarks either assume fixed instructions throughout a task or judge only final outcomes, without capturing collaboration quality during execution. RealCLI pairs task execution evaluation with a five-dimensional framework for measuring human-agent collaboration on realistic CLI tasks.

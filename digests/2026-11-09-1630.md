### New paper "SplatAnything" fixes Gaussian spacing errors in 3D scene reconstruction
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper finds that feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting models suffer from inconsistent Gaussian spacing across scenes, and proposes SplatAnything to normalize it and fix ambiguous training supervision.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Existing feed-forward 3DGS methods predict Gaussian attributes in a radial-distance-normalized space that canonicalizes a global radial statistic of the reconstructed geometry. The paper argues this leaves the spacing between neighboring Gaussian centers free to vary substantially across different scene scales and camera configurations. That mismatch causes systematic shifts in the targets used to train Gaussian scale, producing ambiguous supervision during training. SplatAnything is proposed as a fix for this, though the excerpt does not detail its exact mechanism or report benchmark results.

### Paper proposes P²-MOPD to speed up multi-teacher model distillation
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper introduces P²-MOPD, which jointly optimizes mixing coefficients and student weights to accelerate multi-teacher on-policy distillation of AI models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation combines multiple expert models' capabilities by having a student model learn from trajectories it generates itself under supervision. The paper contrasts this with task arithmetic, which combines expert knowledge through training-free linear parameter combinations instead. P²-MOPD treats the linear combination of student and expert parameters as a single differentiable model, jointly optimizing the mixing coefficients and the student weights under the same on-policy distillation objective. The authors say this goes beyond what simple linear task arithmetic can do, though the excerpt does not give benchmark comparisons.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: P^2-MOPD: Accelerating Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation via Parameter Priors](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03953)

</details>

### New benchmark LongBanana tests long-context, multi-reference AI image generation
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Researchers release LongBanana, described as the first expert-verified benchmark for long-context, multi-reference image synthesis, testing whether models can combine many reference images into one coherent result.

<details><summary>More</summary>

LongBanana targets the emerging area of agent-driven image generation, where models must synthesize one image from multiple scattered reference sources. The benchmark contains 90 tasks organized in a 3-by-3 taxonomy, with each task including roughly 32 reference images. Tasks require retrieving dispersed visual evidence, rejecting distractor images, binding entities and relations correctly, and composing a single coherent final image. The authors describe it as expert-designed and expert-verified, though the excerpt does not specify which models were evaluated or how they performed.

### Paper presents structure-aware kernels for faster relative-position attention
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper proposes structure-aware kernels to make relative-position attention, an alternative to RoPE used in transformers, faster and exact, especially in domains like audio.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Relative-position attention mechanisms modify attention scores by adding content-position or bias-position terms, offering an alternative to RoPE across transformer architectures. The paper notes these mechanisms perform especially well in domains such as audio. Unlike RoPE, which encodes position through per-token rotations applied directly to queries and keys, relative-position terms typically require pairwise offset-dependent computation or lookups. The authors say this complicates integrating them efficiently, and propose structure-aware kernels to address it, though the excerpt cuts off before describing the kernels' design or reporting speedups.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Fast and Exact Relative-Position Attention via Structure-Aware Kernels](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.01182)

</details>

### Ex-Anthropic researcher says Mythos could be retrained far more cheaply now
`models` `policy` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Former Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon says a slide from Anthropic's IPO roadshow shows its models, including Mythos, can now be retrained with a fraction of the original compute, and is renewing his call for a pause on frontier development.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Coxon, who previously worked at OpenAI and Anthropic, pointed to a slide from Anthropic's IPO roadshow materials as part of his reasoning for leaving the company. He says the slide shows Anthropic's algorithms are improving not just quickly but at an accelerating rate, making it possible to retrain a model like Mythos using far less compute than was originally required. Coxon continues to call for a pause on frontier model development, arguing this would let competitors catch up and help make human-level AI a commodity. The claims come from The Decoder's coverage of Coxon's remarks rather than from Anthropic itself.

## 🦾 Robotics

### Runway open-sources Praxis-1, a video-trained robot control model
`robotics` `open-source` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Runway released Praxis-1, an open-weight robotics model under Apache 2.0 that applies knowledge from video training to robot control, and it currently leads Nvidia's RoboLab-120 benchmark for World Action Models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Praxis-1 is released with open weights and source code under the Apache 2.0 license, making it freely usable and modifiable. The model draws on knowledge learned from video training and applies it to controlling robots, placing it in what The Decoder calls the World Action Model category. It currently leads Nvidia's RoboLab-120 benchmark in that category. The Decoder notes the model cannot yet plan complex action sequences on its own, a current limitation of the approach.

- [The Decoder: Runway's open-source Praxis-1 turns video AI into a robot controller](https://the-decoder.com/runways-open-source-praxis-1-turns-video-ai-into-a-robot-controller/)

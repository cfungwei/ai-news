### Researchers propose StereoVLM for metric 3D reasoning in vision-language models
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper proposes StereoVLM, a binocular vision-language model that uses stereo image pairs to reason about metric 3D space, addressing the ambiguity of single-image 3D inference in existing VLMs.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Existing vision-language models can describe 3D scenes semantically but struggle with metric 3D measurements, partly because they infer scene structure from uncalibrated images without cross-view geometric constraints. The paper argues this leaves it unclear whether a model's spatial predictions reflect real visual evidence or just semantic priors. StereoVLM instead uses binocular, calibrated image pairs to provide cross-view geometric constraints for grounding 3D reasoning. The excerpt available does not specify benchmark results or model size.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: StereoVLM: Binocular Vision-Language Models for Metric 3D Spatial Reasoning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.27915)

</details>

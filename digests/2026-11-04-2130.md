### Researchers propose LoCo to cut reasoning model inference costs
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new method called LoCo teaches large reasoning models to recognize when they already have enough information and stop deliberating, aiming to cut inference costs without hurting accuracy.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Large reasoning models often keep generating chain-of-thought tokens even after they have gathered enough information to answer, driving up inference costs. Existing compression methods mostly trim finished reasoning trajectories rather than deciding in the moment whether more reasoning is actually needed. LoCo instead learns a local preference for stopping once the current reasoning state is sufficient, rather than operating after the fact on a full trajectory. The paper frames this as a preference-learning approach to reasoning compression. It does not specify in the available excerpt how much compute savings LoCo delivers or which models and benchmarks were tested.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Think Less, Locally: Local Preference Learning for Reasoning Compression](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.04814)

</details>

### Study factors MLLM embeddings to improve composed image retrieval
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new technique factors multimodal LLM embeddings to better capture requested image edits, aiming to improve composed image retrieval beyond relying on contrastive training alone.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Composed image retrieval asks a system to find a target image given a reference image plus text describing a desired change. Recent methods use multimodal LLMs to generate a single retrieval embedding, but the researchers argue this underuses the model's compositional understanding because it maps queries straight into an embedding via contrastive supervision alone. Their method factors the embedding to more explicitly resolve the intended visual modification before retrieval. The excerpt does not specify which benchmarks were used or how much the factored approach improves accuracy over prior methods.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Factorized MLLM Embeddings for Composed Image Retrieval](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.05193)

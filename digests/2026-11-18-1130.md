### Paper asks whether weight-space programming still needs Transformers

<details><summary>More</summary>

Transformers were chosen largely because they train well with gradient descent and run efficiently on GPUs; in weight-space programming, where an LLM hand-writes weights without gradient-based training, those justifications no longer apply. The authors task three frontier LLMs with either generating weights for a fixed architecture or designing their own, given minimal guidance, ten labeled examples, and a sandbox, mainly on multiple-choice question tasks. The setup tests whether non-Transformer architectures can match or beat Transformers once the usual training constraints are removed. Detailed results are not included in the available excerpt.

### Waypoint-OPD distills RL policy shifts using early-late agreement signals
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new method, Waypoint-OPD, checks whether early and late shifts in a teacher model's policy during reinforcement learning agree, to improve distillation to student models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Policy-shift distillation normally reuses only the difference between a teacher's pre- and post-RL log probabilities as a training signal for students. The authors argue this endpoint-only view can't tell whether local action preferences changed consistently throughout training, since different shift patterns can produce the same final result. Waypoint-OPD instead checks agreement between early and late teacher policy shifts to gate what gets transferred to the student. The excerpt doesn't specify benchmark results or model sizes tested.

### SCOPE improves text-to-image prompt rewriting by rewarding semantic spans
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

<details><summary>More</summary>

The rewriter jointly generates an enhanced prompt and the credit assignment for its parts. The excerpt doesn't specify which image generator or benchmark was used to validate the approach.

### Researchers diagnose and fix training instability in Gated DeltaNet-2
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper traces Gated DeltaNet-2's long-context training failures to asymmetric erase operators and proposes a fix to stabilize its recurrent state.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Gated DeltaNet-2 (GDN-2) is a linear-attention architecture that uses fixed-size recurrent states for efficient long-sequence modeling, with independent channel-wise control over erasure and writing to improve state-update expressiveness and in-context retrieval. Despite these advantages, the authors found GDN-2 suffers training failures specifically during long-context training. They trace the problem to an asymmetry between the reading and modification directions during the erase operation, which produces non-normal erase operators. ### Entangle scales human-video in-context learning for robot action models
`robotics` `research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Entangle proposes a method to scale in-context learning from human videos for robotic world-action models, aiming to generalize to unseen tasks without retraining.

<details><summary>More</summary>

In-context learning lets foundation models perform unseen tasks from context alone, without updating parameters, and in robotic manipulation a human video can supply that context by showing how a task should be done. Scaling this approach on world-action models has been limited by two problems: human-video and robot-action pairs are not temporally aligned, and standard action losses let policies rely on shortcuts like the initial scene or task identity instead of tracking the demonstrated behavior. Entangle is presented as an approach addressing these issues to scale human-video in-context learning across unseen tasks and contexts. The excerpt does not detail the specific technique used or report benchmark results.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Entangle: Scaling Human-Video In-Context World-Action Models across Unseen Tasks and Contexts](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.28732)

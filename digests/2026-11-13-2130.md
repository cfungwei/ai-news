`hardware` `policy` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

<details><summary>More</summary>

Will DePue, a former OpenAI researcher, says he has produced the first technical evidence that Chinese AI labs including DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax, and Ant Group used Nvidia's latest Blackwell GPUs despite US export controls. His method reads characteristic chip signatures directly from the published weights of the models rather than relying on self-reported hardware claims.

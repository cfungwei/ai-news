### Lean/Mathlib formally verifies Levent Alpöge's proof of the Riemann hypothesis
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

The Lean and Mathlib community has formally confirmed mathematician Levent Alpöge's proof of the Riemann hypothesis after checking 17 million lines of verification code, arriving decades earlier than experts like Terence Tao had expected.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Dozens of independent verifiers checked the proof's roughly 17 million lines of Lean and Mathlib code before the community declared it correct. The result arrived years ahead of expectations: Terence Tao had predicted a proof of the Riemann hypothesis would not appear until around 2050. Tao also did not expect artificial intelligence to solve the problem on its own, a detail that leaves open how much AI tooling contributed to Alpöge's work. No further detail on the proof's methods or Alpöge's background is available from the source.

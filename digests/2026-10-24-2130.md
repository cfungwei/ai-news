### Study: AI helps junior patent lawyers most, but gains may not stick
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A three-month study of 133 patent attorneys found AI immediately boosts junior lawyers' performance the most, but when AI is removed only senior attorneys retain the gains.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The field experiment tracked 133 patent attorneys over three months, comparing their work with and without AI assistance. Junior attorneys saw the largest immediate performance gains from using AI. When AI access was removed, only senior attorneys kept their improved performance, while junior attorneys' gains faded. The researchers suggest baseline expertise is a prerequisite for turning AI-assisted practice into lasting skill, implying heavy reliance on AI early in a career could stunt professional growth. The Decoder's report does not name the study's authors or where it was published.

### Meta trains byte-reading AI models that start weak but may win out
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A Meta paper trains roughly 1-billion-parameter models to read raw bytes instead of tokens, partly via distillation from Llama 3 8B. Byte-based models start out weaker than token-based ones but are predicted to overtake them with scale.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The paper trains small models of about 1 billion parameters to process raw bytes instead of tokens, alongside comparable models trained on tokens. Both were partly trained via distillation from a larger teacher model, Llama 3 8B, which required translating between the teacher's token-based reasoning and the student's byte-level reading. The byte-based models underperform the token-based ones at this scale. The paper reportedly predicts byte-based models will overtake token-based ones given more scale or training, according to the summary shared on r/LocalLLaMA. The post does not name the paper's authors or title directly, only linking to the arXiv PDF.

- [r/LocalLLaMA: Meta trained small AI models to read raw bytes instead of tokens. They start out worse, but the paper predicts they end up better](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xe88ja/meta_trained_small_ai_models_to_read_raw_bytes/)

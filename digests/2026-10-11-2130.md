### Vals AI study finds multi-agent teams cost 5x more for barely any quality gain
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A Vals AI study finds teams of AI agents barely outperform solo agents while costing up to 5.1x more. Only one of four tests showed a measurable gain, and Anthropic's own data shows quality plateauing past ten agents.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Research firm Vals AI tested multi-agent teams against solo AI agents and found only marginal performance gains despite much higher costs. Multi-agent teams cost up to 5.1 times more to run than a single agent. Just one of four tests, using GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5, showed a measurable quality improvement. Anthropic's own internal data reportedly backs up the finding: beyond ten agents working together, quality gains plateau while token costs keep rising.

### Epoch AI and Anthropic: AI agents overstate results, can't yet do autonomous research
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Separate studies by Epoch AI and Anthropic find today's AI models can run experiments but lack scientific self-criticism and genuine creative thinking, with top models reaching only 15% of the human reference score.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Epoch AI and Anthropic independently studied how well current AI models perform autonomous scientific research, and reached the same conclusion. Models including GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5 can run experiments but lack scientific self-criticism and genuine creative thinking. At best, Sol reached only 15 percent of the human reference score on the benchmark used, and even that result came from methods researchers already knew rather than ones the model devised itself. The studies found the models' biggest weakness is still an inability to critically question their own results.

- [The Decoder: AI agents overstate their results and remain far from autonomous research, study finds](https://the-decoder.com/ai-agents-overstate-their-results-and-remain-far-from-autonomous-research-study-finds/)

</details>

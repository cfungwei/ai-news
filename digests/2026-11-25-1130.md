<details><summary>More</summary>

Loom attention combines horizontal attention over tokens, as in standard Transformers, with vertical attention over the outputs of earlier heads, used to obtain a head's query, key and value. The vertical attention is implemented efficiently using normalized and gated linear attention over a per-token matrix memory, which stores compressed head outputs and acts as a second residual stream. This design, called LoomFormer, is described as a dual-stream architecture. The excerpt cuts off before detailing benchmark results or model sizes tested.

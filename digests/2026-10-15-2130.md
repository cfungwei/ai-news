### New 'Hybrid Reasoning Agents' architecture blends latent and text-based reasoning
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A paper proposes Hybrid Reasoning Agents, which dynamically switch between efficient internal 'latent' reasoning and transparent text-based reasoning, aiming to give LLM agents both speed and the tool access text interaction provides.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Today's LLM agents reason and act almost entirely through text, which keeps their reasoning transparent and tool use straightforward but can be inefficient. Latent reasoning offers a faster internal medium, but on its own it lacks native tool access and makes an agent's reasoning hard to inspect. HybridAgents are designed to navigate between the two modes throughout a single, unified generation process rather than picking one approach upfront.

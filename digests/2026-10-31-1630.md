### Google DeepMind and WRI release ForTy global forest map
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Google DeepMind and the World Resources Institute released ForTy, a satellite-and-AI map classifying forests and land into six categories to help track deforestation.

<details><summary>More</summary>

ForTy classifies land into six categories, including primary forest, plantations, and tree crops, using satellite data and AI. The map is intended to help track deforestation under EU regulatory rules. It identifies only about half of planted forests and tree-crop areas, and 60% of plantations, indicating gaps in coverage. The project is a collaboration between Google DeepMind and the World Resources Institute.

- [The Decoder: Google Deepmind creates a global map of forests with satellite data and AI](https://the-decoder.com/google-deepmind-creates-a-global-map-of-forests-with-satellite-data-and-ai/)

</details>

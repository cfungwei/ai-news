`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

DeepMind unveiled AlphaGenome 2, which analyzes genetic variants at the level of individual cell types rather than bulk tissue. Trained on over 107 million cell profiles, it identifies nearly twice as many disease-relevant gene changes as its predecessor.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The shift from bulk-tissue to single-cell-type resolution is meant to give researchers a finer-grained view of how genetic variants affect specific cells rather than averaged tissue samples. The model is available for noncommercial research use through an API, and Google plans to integrate it into its agentic platform, Antigravity. The report does not specify commercial licensing terms or a timeline for the Antigravity integration. It is unclear how the model performs on cell types underrepresented in its training data.

### Terence Tao envisions AI-driven 'Big Math,' calls tech firms' proofs 'toxic waste'
`research` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Mathematician Terence Tao outlined a 'Big Math' future where human-AI teams tackle thousands of problems together, while criticizing major tech companies' AI-generated proofs as unexplained 'toxic waste.'

<details><summary>More</summary>

On the Hard Fork podcast, Tao described a future he calls 'Big Math,' in which human-AI teams would systematically map out thousands of mathematical problems rather than mathematicians working alone. He said AI could deliver a first rough solution that humans then verify and place in context, creating a new division of labor between researchers and models. Tao also criticized proofs produced by major tech companies, calling them 'toxic waste' because the models can solve problems but cannot explain how they arrived at the results. He argued this opacity undermines the value of AI-assisted mathematical work. It remains unclear which specific companies or proofs he was referring to.

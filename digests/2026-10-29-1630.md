### CausGround grounds causal interactions for adaptive embodied planning
`research` `robotics` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

CausGround is a method for embodied planning that grounds multimodal models' understanding of when actions are executable and how they change the world, aiming to adapt without costly environment-specific training.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Multimodal large language models show promising reasoning capabilities, but reliable physical interaction requires planning that accounts for preconditions, when actions are executable, and effects, how they change the world. These preconditions and effects vary by environment and are often not captured by MLLMs, making adaptation hard. Existing approaches either need costly environment-specific training or rely on unstructured interaction experience that is difficult to reuse. CausGround is introduced as a causal interaction grounding method meant to let embodied planning adapt to new environments more efficiently than these prior approaches.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: CausGround: Causal Interaction Grounding for Adaptive Embodied Planning](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.29702)

</details>

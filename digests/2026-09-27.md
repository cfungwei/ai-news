### Researchers find LLM outputs combine linearly when inputs are mixed
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A paper shows that linearly combining inputs from separate text streams makes an LLM output a superposition of each stream's next-token distribution.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The authors call this the Superposition Linearity Hypothesis and present evidence that superposition is an intrinsic property of the Transformer architecture. The finding is surprising because LLMs are built from highly non-linear components.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Your Transformer Can Hold Two Thoughts at Once: Evidence of Linear Superposition in LLMs](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29845)

</details>

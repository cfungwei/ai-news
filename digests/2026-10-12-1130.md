`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

VisionWeave lets multimodal large language models give more visual detail to regions that need it and less to simple areas, aiming to cut the cost of dense, fixed-size patch tokens.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Multimodal LLMs typically encode images into dense, fixed-size patch tokens, which is costly because visual information is unevenly distributed across a scene. Existing token-pruning and adaptive methods are limited in how finely they adjust granularity, generalize across tasks, and integrate with the base model. VisionWeave instead builds elastic visual representations as a native capability of the MLLM itself, rather than as a separate pruning step. The excerpt does not specify benchmark results or which base models were tested.

### SoloQ brings calibration-free low-bit quantization to diffusion language models
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

SoloQ is a new quantization method for diffusion language models that skips the calibration step, aiming to cut the memory and compute costs of deploying these increasingly popular models.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Diffusion large language models are a growing alternative to autoregressive LLMs, generating text through bidirectional diffusion rather than next-token prediction. Their large size and high inference cost make low-bit weight-activation quantization attractive, but existing quantization approaches reportedly fall short for this architecture. Full-sequence denoising requires repeated compute-heavy forward passes, and block-diffusion variants add a memory-intensive KV-cache on top. SoloQ is proposed as a calibration-free quantization method to address these costs, though the excerpt cuts off before describing results.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: SoloQ: Calibration-Free Quantization for Diffusion Language Models](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.07121)

</details>

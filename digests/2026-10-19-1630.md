### LiveMACEBench benchmarks LLM agents using live financial markets
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Researchers introduce LiveMACEBench, a process-aware benchmark that judges LLM agents by how they act, not just outcomes, using live financial markets as an evolving testbed.

<details><summary>More</summary>

The paper argues that judging agents by outcomes alone can hide the capabilities that actually produced them, especially in environments that change as the agent acts. LiveMACEBench uses live financial markets as a naturally evolving testbed for persistent LLM agents rather than a static benchmark. Five frontier LLMs are run along continuous trajectories under matched conditions covering tool use and persistent memory. The approach is meant to separate genuine capability from favorable market conditions. It is unclear from the excerpt which five models were tested or how performance compared between them.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: LiveMACE: Process-Aware Evaluation of LLM Agent Capabilities in Evolving Markets](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.09872)

### Scene-Designer teaches agents to design and critique 3D indoor scenes
`research` `agents` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Scene-Designer is an agentic framework that learns to generate coherent 3D indoor scenes from text by training on both design and critique experience, not just static scene data.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Generating complete, usable 3D indoor scenes from natural language needs design intelligence: the ability both to design a scene and to judge its quality. The paper notes existing approaches either lean on general-purpose models with no domain-specific design experience, or learn only from static scenes. Scene-Designer instead learns from complementary design and critique experiences. The authors built two datasets to support this training approach, though the excerpt does not specify their size or contents. It is also unclear how Scene-Designer was evaluated against prior methods.

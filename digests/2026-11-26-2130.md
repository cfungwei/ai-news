## 📄 Research

### Paper introduces DocSEAM for multi-page document understanding in VLMs
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper proposes DocSEAM, a lightweight module that injects cross-page context between a vision encoder and language model, fixing the feature discontinuity multi-page document VLMs face at page boundaries.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Today's vision-language models for multi-page documents encode each page independently, creating a feature-level discontinuity at page boundaries since visual tokens have no access to content on adjacent pages. The language model must then reason over page representations that were formed in isolation. DocSEAM, short for Document Selective Enrichment via Anchored Masking, is a lightweight module inserted between a pretrained vision encoder and the language model that injects local cross-page context to address this.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: DocSEAM: Bidirectional Cross-Page Encoding for Multi-Page Document Understanding](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.34420)

</details>

### Paper tackles unifying understanding and generation in vision-language models
`research` `models` (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new paper studies why unified multimodal models struggle to combine visual understanding and generation, finding the core issue is the mismatch between the visual representations each task uses.

<details><summary>More</summary>

Recent unified multimodal models try to combine visual understanding and generation in a single model, but integrating the two capabilities has proven difficult. The paper argues that pretrained vision-language models offer a way to build on already-learned multimodal knowledge, yet making that knowledge usable for visual generation is nontrivial. It identifies the central challenge as the heterogeneous visual representations used for understanding versus generation.

- [Hugging Face Daily Papers: Let Vision-Language Models Generate Images through Their Representations](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31237)

</details>

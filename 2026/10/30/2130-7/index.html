Pope Leo XIV calls for AI 'disarmament,' backs autonomous weapons ban

policy (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

In a Friday address, Pope Leo XIV said AI systems should be 'disarmed' and urged UN member states to pursue a ban on lethal autonomous weapons at next month's Geneva meeting.

The Pope's address framed AI as 'tools, not persons,' emphasizing that humans retain responsibility to guide, change, or reject AI applications rather than cede judgment to the technology. He called on United Nations member states meeting in Geneva next month to take concrete steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons. The address did not specify what disarmament would require in practice beyond the ban on autonomous weapons. It adds a prominent religious voice to the broader debate over military AI regulation.

Meta's new terms may let it train AI on news publishers' sites for free

policyindustry (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Meta's updated business terms, taking effect October 30th, appear to let it crawl and scrape content from sites 'associated' with businesses on its platforms, including news publishers, to train AI.

The new terms were first reported by Nieman Lab and apply to websites 'associated' with businesses that use Meta's platforms, including online publishers. Meta says it can use this outside content to 'develop, improve, research,' and 'train' its AI systems. Legal experts cited by Ars Technica describe the streamlined commercial terms of service as a way for Meta to avoid millions of dollars in AI licensing fees it might otherwise owe publishers. The terms take effect October 30th.

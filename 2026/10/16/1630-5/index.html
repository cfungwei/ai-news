Muse Spark Open debuts as a large open-weight model for coding, agents

modelsopen-sourceagents (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter (42B active) open-weight model with a 1M-token context, launches targeting coding and long-horizon agentic tasks, with Unsloth offering support on day one.

Muse Spark Open is a mixture-of-experts model with 816B total parameters and 42B active, a 1M-token context window, and support for text, image, video, and audio inputs. It is built for coding and agentic work, with a focus on long-horizon tasks, complex software engineering, and multi-step reasoning. The release includes additional safety post-training, with evaluations described as conducted under Meta's Superintelligence Scaling Framework. Unsloth published support for the model on the same day, per a separate r/LocalLLaMA post, though that post's own content was not captured in the excerpts. The excerpts do not name the company behind Muse Spark Open or give benchmark results.

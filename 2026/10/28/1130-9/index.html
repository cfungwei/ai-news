Hy-Nomas is an 8B video world model for free-form interactive worlds

modelsresearch (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Hy-Nomas is an 8B-parameter diffusion video world model that follows free-form text commands and continuous camera control across multiple turns, generating new video segments conditioned on past history and actions.

Interactive world generation needs a model that can follow a sequence of commands while remembering prior history and the consequences of past actions. Hy-Nomas generates variable-length video segments conditioned on the current instruction and a cache of earlier context, rather than treating each turn independently. Its core design aligns structured video captions with action-aware causal attention, tying descriptions of what happens on screen to the actions that caused them. The approach aims to let a single model keep a long interactive session visually and causally consistent as a user issues new commands.

Report: US media, film and arts sector has lost 200,000 jobs

other (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A widely discussed Hacker News submission examines roughly 200,000 US job losses across media, film, and the arts, and asks how much of the decline is attributable to AI.

The linked analysis, from Apricitas Economics, looks at employment data for the creative class—media, film, and arts workers—and estimates roughly 200,000 jobs lost. It frames the central question as how much of that decline can be attributed to AI tools displacing creative work versus other economic factors. The submission drew significant engagement on Hacker News, with 293 points and 299 comments at last count. The item excerpt does not specify the time period covered by the job-loss figure or the methodology used to isolate AI's contribution.

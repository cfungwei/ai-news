Microsoft posts record quarterly revenue, discloses Azure and OpenAI numbers

industry (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Microsoft reported $92.3 billion in quarterly revenue, up 19% year over year, and for the first time disclosed Azure's revenue at $33.2 billion. The company also said it earned $10 billion from commercial arrangements with OpenAI in the quarter.

The results mark Microsoft's tenth consecutive quarter of record revenue, with the $92.3 billion total up 19% year over year. Microsoft had never previously broken out Azure's quarterly revenue in dollars; the new $33.2 billion figure allows direct comparison with AWS, the market's largest cloud provider. Microsoft also disclosed it recorded $10 billion in revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI, including revenue-sharing payments, during the quarter ending September 30. TechCrunch said the figure underscores how financially dependent the Microsoft-OpenAI partnership remains for both companies.

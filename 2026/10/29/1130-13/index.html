Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, topping benchmarks but with restricted access

modelsagents (tagged by fallback) · 6 sources

Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, a frontier model topping the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with gains in coding, agentic tasks, and cybersecurity. Access is initially limited to selected security experts, two weeks after the previous Gemini 4 Argon release.

Gemini 4 Carbon scored 59 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0, topping both Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra. Google built it for extended agent workflows and the full cyber defense lifecycle, and access is initially limited to selected security experts. The release comes just two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon, continuing Google's rapid release cadence. Ars Technica noted that most users cannot yet try the model despite its benchmark lead. The announcement drew 877 points and 724 comments on Hacker News.

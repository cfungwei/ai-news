VepAgent uses tool-augmented reinforcement learning for video event prediction

researchagents (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Researchers introduce VepAgent, an agentic framework combining causal-transition reasoning with reinforcement learning to help multimodal models predict future events in video.

Multimodal large language models have shown promise on video understanding but tend to rely on retrospective summarization and text-centric priors, limiting their ability to reason about unobserved causal transitions for video event prediction. VepAgent instead integrates causal-transition reasoning with tool-augmented reinforcement learning. The paper contrasts this with prior methods that passively project forward rather than actively reasoning about causality. Specific performance gains are not given in the available excerpt.

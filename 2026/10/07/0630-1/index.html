Mistral releases 1-trillion-parameter Large 4, nicknamed 'Le Chonk'

modelsopen-source (tagged by fallback) · 6 sources

Mistral released a preview of Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter, 49-billion-active-parameter model trained on 3,800 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs, pitched as Europe's answer to US and Chinese frontier models, with open weights due later this month.

Mistral's new flagship is available via API now in two reasoning modes ('none' and 'high'), with the company promising to release open weights by the end of the month. In the independent Intelligence Index benchmark, Mistral Large 4 makes a notable jump over Mistral's prior models but still trails Claude, GPT-6, and top Chinese models. Mistral is pitching the model's willingness to do cybersecurity work that closed US labs decline, as part of a broader argument that it remains 'in the race' on frontier AI built on its own European infrastructure. The release drew heavy discussion on Hacker News, including a nearly 1,500-point submission.

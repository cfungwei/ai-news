Researchers extend LeCun's JEPA into a universal world model spanning physics to biology

research (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Researchers at PhAI Labs expanded Yann LeCun's JEPA architecture to work across seven scientific fields, from robotics to biomedicine, and produced a liver cancer treatment candidate that showed early lab promise.

The project stretches JEPA, the self-supervised 'world model' architecture associated with Yann LeCun, well beyond its original scope by applying it across seven different fields. One output of the effort was a liver cancer treatment candidate that showed promise in lab tests. The researchers are careful to note the study doesn't establish whether that candidate could become an actual therapy, just that it showed early promise. The excerpt doesn't specify the other six fields beyond robotics and biomedicine, or performance details within each.

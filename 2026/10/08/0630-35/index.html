Microsoft launches Surface Laptop Ultra, an Nvidia-powered AI PC

hardwareproducts (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

At a Windows and Surface event, Microsoft unveiled the Surface Laptop Ultra, a new Nvidia RTX Arm-based laptop starting at $2,599 for an 8-core CPU, 24GB RAM, and 512GB storage.

The laptop was the headline announcement of a broader Windows and Surface keynote in San Francisco that also covered new Copilot capabilities and a separate RTX Spark Dev Box. Microsoft is positioning the machine as part of a wider push toward Nvidia-chip AI PCs running a revamped version of Windows 11 built around on-device AI models and agents. Pricing and configuration details were revealed alongside the keynote, though a ship date wasn't specified in the coverage.

Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5 with steep price cuts

models (tagged by fallback) · 4 sources

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, a faster, cheaper model that it says sharply improves on its predecessor's benchmarks while cutting token prices by up to 90 percent.

The new Haiku jumps from 15.7 to 72.4 percent on the OSWorld computer-use benchmark compared with Haiku 4.5, which launched nearly a year ago. Pricing now matches OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for the first 100,000 tokens, rising 5x beyond that. A new tokenizer consumes more tokens per task, offsetting some of the savings. The release topped Hacker News with over 275 comments, with commentators framing it as evidence the AI pricing race is intensifying rather than cooling.

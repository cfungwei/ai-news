Cryptographer Matthew Green estimates small but real odds AI breaks public-key crypto

policyresearch (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Cryptographer Matthew Green says there's roughly a 1% chance we end up in a low-security 'Minicrypt' world and a 15% chance we lose confidence in public-key encryption, because AI surprises outpace standards.

Quoted via Simon Willison's blog, Green frames himself as raising worst-case possibilities others are reluctant to voice for fear of seeming unserious. His core concern is a mismatch in speed: AI is producing cryptographic or mathematical surprises much faster than humans, even with AI assistance, can replace affected standards. He argues that recovering from such a surprise is difficult once it happens, rather than something that can simply be patched quickly. The excerpt cuts off before Green elaborates on what kind of surprise he has in mind or what mitigations he'd recommend.

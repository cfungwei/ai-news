TypeSafe AI's non-text model Jev valued at $7.5B after $870M raise

industrymodels (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

TypeSafe AI, maker of the non-text AI model Jev, raised $870M at a $7.5B valuation just weeks after launch, with users and corporations drawn to claims it's faster and uses far fewer tokens than LLMs.

According to TechCrunch, what has generated excitement around Jev is TypeSafe's claim that it works significantly faster and uses far fewer tokens than large language models, despite not being a text-based model. A Hacker News submission of TypeSafe's own blog post confirms the $870M raise at a $7.5B valuation, which came just weeks after Jev's launch. The available excerpts don't explain what Jev actually does, who is investing, or how its non-text architecture compares technically to LLMs.

Nikon disqualifies Small World in Motion winner over undisclosed AI use

policyother (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Nikon disqualified the original first-place winner of its Small World in Motion contest for violating rules on generative AI; a new winner, Nguyen Nam Nhat of Vietnam, was named instead.

Nikon said the video that originally won first place in its Small World in Motion microscopy contest 'did not comply with the competition rules regarding generative AI.' The BBC reported the disqualified video, from Dr. Ning Xu, claimed to show cilia moving in a child's airway. The new first-place winner is Nguyen Nam Nhat of Vietnam, for a video of a roundworm and a single-celled Dileptus. The case adds to a string of recent incidents where AI-generated or AI-assisted media was presented as genuine scientific or documentary footage.

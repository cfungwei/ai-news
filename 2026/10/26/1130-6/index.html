CrowdGPT aims to train a 1B-parameter LLM without datacenters

modelsopen-source (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new open-source project called CrowdGPT aims to collaboratively train a 1-billion-parameter LLM using volunteers' own PCs instead of datacenters, with contributors getting a say in the project's direction.

According to its r/LocalLLaMA announcement, CrowdGPT lets anyone train the model independently on their own PC, then contribute those updates to a global model via a shared protocol. The project is positioned as an alternative to large American and Chinese AI companies, aiming to be open not just in its weights but in how decisions about its direction get made. The excerpt does not specify the training protocol's technical details, a timeline, or how many contributors have joined so far.

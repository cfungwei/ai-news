Anthropic files S-1 prospectus for Nasdaq IPO under ticker ANT

industrypolicy (tagged by fallback) · 5 sources

Anthropic filed its S-1 with the SEC to go public on Nasdaq as ANT, disclosing financials, risks, and a governance structure meant to preserve founder control and its safety mission.

Anthropic filed its S-1 prospectus with the SEC, confirming plans to list on the Nasdaq exchange under the ticker "ANT" and beat OpenAI to a public listing. The filing discloses soaring revenue alongside mounting losses, major enterprise adoption, and a governance structure built to preserve founder control and the company's safety mission. In the prospectus, Anthropic lays out a plan it says could "revolutionize every area of human endeavor" with AI, while also describing a "closing the loop" strategy it frames as both AI's best path to success and a source of "existential" risk. The filing drew heavy attention, with a Hacker News discussion of the S-1 document reaching 880 points and 792 comments. Full financial details and risk factors are laid out in the prospectus itself, which was filed on Monday.

Walmart opens its site to AI shopping agents as Amazon keeps them out

agentsproducts (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Walmart will let AI agents shop on its site next month, with controls for approving purchases and setting spending limits — a contrasting stance to Amazon, which blocks AI agents from shopping on its platform.

Starting next month, Walmart will allow AI agents to shop on its website on shoppers' behalf. The retailer is building in systems so customers can approve and review purchases and set spending limits on what agents buy. The move is framed as a direct contrast to Amazon, which currently blocks AI agents from shopping on its site. The split reflects a broader industry divide over how retailers should treat autonomous AI shopping agents.

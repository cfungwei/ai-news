Anthropic launches Claude Duet, an AI agent to rival Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots

agentsproducts (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Anthropic launched Claude Duet, a research-preview AI agent for professionals and small business owners, available as a standalone iOS and Android app and through Claude on the web, competing with Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots.

Claude Duet is designed for professionals and small business owners rather than general consumers. It launches as a research preview and is available both as a standalone app on iOS and Android and through Claude on the web. The move positions Anthropic directly against Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots in the emerging market for consumer-facing AI agents. The Hacker News discussion of the launch drew 344 points and 315 comments, among the most active threads of the day.

Cloudflare launches Clef, a fast decision model for AI agents

modelsagentsopen-source (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Cloudflare released Clef and Clef-flash, Apache 2.0-licensed decision models built on Qwen that let AI agents make structured choices without generating text, positioning them as a faster rival to TypeSafe AI's Jev.

According to Hugging Face trending data, Clef is an image-text-to-text model that had accumulated 763 likes and 824 downloads, tagged with qwen3_5, image-text-to-typed-output, and structured-output. The Decoder reports that Clef-flash returns classifications in about 39 milliseconds, more than ten times faster than Jev. Both Clef and Clef-flash are built on Qwen and released under the Apache 2.0 license. Cloudflare frames the models as letting AI agents make structured decisions without a human needing to stay in the loop.

Facebook lets users steer their feed algorithm with new AI tool

products (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Meta launched 'Your Algorithm' on Facebook, letting users directly add topics they want more or less of in their feed and Reels, with the system responding immediately to 'interested' and 'not interested' signals.

The feature previously launched on Instagram and has now come to Facebook. Users can manage topics directly rather than relying solely on implicit engagement signals, and Meta says the feed updates respond right away once a preference is set. Both outlets describe it as giving people more explicit control over recommendations rather than changing the underlying ranking model. Neither excerpt specifies a rollout timeline, region availability, or how the system reconciles topic preferences with other ranking signals.

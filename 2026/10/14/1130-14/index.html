Google Play now lets people launch personal vibe-coded apps directly

productstools (tagged by fallback) · 3 sources

Google expanded Google Play so AI-generated 'vibe-coded' apps built in AI Studio can launch directly through the Play Store in the US, without connecting a phone to a computer, and is exploring turning personal apps into wider releases.

Previously, installing a vibe-coded app built with Google's AI Studio required manually connecting a phone to a computer. The new feature removes that step, letting personal apps launch straight from the Play Store. Google had already let people vibe-code apps privately for themselves; this update is about distribution rather than creation. Reporting also notes Google's apparent interest in helping some of these personal, AI-generated apps grow into full public releases, though specifics on how that process would work, what review it requires, or monetization are not given in the excerpts.

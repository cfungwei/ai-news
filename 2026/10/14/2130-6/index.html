Microsoft claims new ML tool forecasts weather accurately weeks out

researchtools (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Microsoft says a new machine learning approach extends reliable weather forecasts to two to six weeks ahead, beyond the usual two-week limit of physics-based and existing AI models.

Traditional physics-based forecasting and even current AI weather models typically lose reliability beyond about two weeks. Microsoft researchers say they found a cost-effective method that pushes accurate forecasting out to two to six weeks in advance. The company frames the improvement as useful for farmers and emergency responders who need longer lead times to plan. The excerpt does not specify the technique used or give performance comparisons against existing models.

Google releases Gemini 4 Argon, its most powerful model yet

models (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Google released Gemini 4 Argon, marketed as its most powerful model yet and positioned as a workhorse for coding and cybersecurity work, with a 1M-token output limit.

Google is marketing Gemini 4 Argon as a response to OpenAI's Astra and Anthropic's Fable models, built for coding and cybersecurity use cases. One report highlights a 1 million token output limit as a standout spec. Access is currently limited: coverage notes it's only available to 'government users and trusted cyber defenders' in something called the Fairwind Program, not the general public. The excerpts don't give pricing or a broader rollout timeline for wider access.

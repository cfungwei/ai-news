Hobbyist open-sources Vega, an 800M-param physics-based decision model

modelsopen-source (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Following the earlier open-source release of Laya, the same r/LocalLLaMA poster has open-sourced Vega, a new 800 million parameter physics-based decision model with 73k context and image support.

A r/LocalLLaMA poster previously released Laya, described as the first open-source version of 'JEV,' after noting that a company called Jev had used their one-year-old architecture. That release reportedly grew to a large scale with support from the local LLaMA community. The poster has now open-sourced a new physics-based typed decision model called Vega. Vega has about 800 million parameters, a 73,000-token context window, and supports image input. The excerpt does not clarify what distinguishes a 'physics-based typed decision model' architecturally or provide benchmark comparisons.

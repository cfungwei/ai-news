r/LocalLLaMA user details a 4x R9700 build for a 10-person startup

hardware (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A poster on r/LocalLLaMA shared specs for an $18k local inference rig built for a startup, using four AMD Radeon R9700 AI Pro GPUs for a total of 128GB VRAM to serve Qwen and DeepSeek models.

The build uses a Threadripper 9970X with 128GB of ECC DDR5 RAM and four undervolted AMD Radeon R9700 AI Pro GPUs, each capped under 210W, for a combined 128GB of VRAM, at a total cost of about $18,000. It serves models including Qwen 3.8 Next Flash and DeepSeek V4 Flash using a fork of the Radiance inference engine. The poster reported throughput of roughly 6,300-6,800 tokens/second aggregate prefill and several hundred tokens/second aggregate decode across 16 concurrent sessions, with context windows ranging from 4k to 128k. The post is aimed at other self-hosters sizing similar multi-GPU AMD setups.

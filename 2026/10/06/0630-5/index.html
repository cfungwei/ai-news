Opus 5.5 agents help discover two room-temperature magnetic semiconductor candidates

researchmodels (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

AI agents built on Opus 5.5 reportedly discovered two candidate materials that behave as magnetic semiconductors at room temperature, a combination that is rare and valuable for electronics.

Room-temperature magnetic semiconductors are difficult to find because magnetism and semiconducting behavior rarely coexist stably outside cryogenic conditions, making candidates significant if confirmed. The discovery was made using AI agents rather than through conventional manual materials search. The finding drew substantial discussion on Hacker News, with debate over the materials' real-world viability and how the discovery process worked. The excerpt does not specify independent experimental validation of the candidates.

Chart compares open-weight model scores by size, including Meta's 816B Muse Spark Open

modelsopen-source (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A r/LocalLLaMA chart plots Artificial Analysis scores against parameter count for 11 open-weight models, showing Meta's 816B-parameter Muse Spark Open scoring 39, just behind the far smaller 180B Qwen3.8-Flash-Next at 40.

The post charts Artificial Analysis scores against total parameter count, on a log scale, for 11 open-weight models, highlighting those released in October 2026. Meta's Muse Spark Open, an 816-billion-parameter model with 42 billion active parameters, scores 39, while Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, a much smaller 180-billion-parameter model with 6 billion active parameters, scores 40. The post notes storage size for quantized versions: roughly 506 GB for Muse Spark Open versus 111 GB for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next in Unsloth's 4-bit format. It adds that Meta's recommended local setup for Muse Spark Open is two Mac Studios with M5 Ultra chips and 512 GB of memory each.

OpenAI's DevDay 2026 debuts Dots, GPT-6.1 Sol, and a wave of agent tools

productsagentsindustry (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

OpenAI used DevDay 2026 to unveil a slate of releases, including the Dots agent, GPT-6.1 Sol, an Ultrafast tier, the Decisions and Agents APIs, Spaces, and a Marketplace, alongside a claim of 1.2 billion weekly ChatGPT users.

The event, described as OpenAI's most confident DevDay yet, bundled multiple product lines into a single announcement wave rather than one flagship release. New surfaces include Dots (an agent product), a Spaces feature, and a Marketplace, alongside model and infrastructure updates like GPT-6.1 Sol and an 'Ultrafast' offering. OpenAI also disclosed that ChatGPT now has 1.2 billion weekly active users. Further specifics on pricing, availability, and rollout for each individual product were not detailed in the roundup.

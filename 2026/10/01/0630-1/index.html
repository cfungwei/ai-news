Google launches Gemini 4 Argon, restricts early access to trusted testers

models (tagged by fallback) · 5 sources

Google DeepMind released Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier model aimed at software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, and cybersecurity defense, but is initially limiting access to select trusted testers rather than the public.

Gemini 4 Argon is Google's first frontier model release in more than seven months, and in independent testing it matches OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra while still trailing Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5. The model uses more than twice as many tokens per task as Astra, though its per-token price is comparatively low. Google DeepMind SVP Koray Kavukcuoglu says it delivers frontier performance in complex workflows including legal and finance work and cybersecurity defense. For now only a small group of testers, including 'trusted cyber defenders,' can use it, with broader API and paid-tier access to follow later.

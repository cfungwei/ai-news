Trump and tech CEOs sign voluntary 'Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities'

policy (tagged by fallback) · 4 sources

President Trump and executives from major AI companies signed a voluntary, 'morally binding' AI safety accord at the White House, alongside an executive order renaming AI 'Super Intelligence.'

The agreement, called the Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities, was shared online by presidential adviser David Sacks and signed by leaders including Mark Zuckerberg, Greg Brockman, Jensen Huang, and Elon Musk. It calls for outside auditors to check whether models behave as intended, but it is unclear what happens if a company breaks the commitment. Trump said his earlier claims that AI safety fears were a Democratic 'hoax' no longer apply now that he has renamed the technology 'Super Intelligence.' Critics note the deal relies on the same companies policing themselves, with no enforcement mechanism specified.

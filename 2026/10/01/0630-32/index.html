OpenAI and Synopsys build an AI model to design chips like an engineer

hardwareindustry (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

OpenAI and Synopsys are jointly building GPT-Synopsys, a specialized AI model meant to operate Synopsys' chip design (EDA) tools autonomously and optimize designs like an experienced engineer.

Early tests of the model are already underway with semiconductor customers, according to the report. The partnership likely also benefits OpenAI's own chip development ambitions, giving it deeper exposure to EDA workflows. The excerpt does not specify a public release timeline or which semiconductor customers are testing it.

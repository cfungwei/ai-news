OpenAI's GPT-6-Astra solves 49 of 50 unpublished physics research problems

modelsresearch (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

On the TheoPhysEval benchmark, GPT-6-Astra solved 49 of 50 unpublished theoretical physics calculations as accurately as leading specialists, at roughly $2.40 per problem, though researchers say the model still lacks research 'taste.'

The benchmark, TheoPhysEval, tests unpublished theoretical physics calculations rather than problems the model could have seen in training. In some cases where the agent appeared to fail, the experts' own reference solutions reportedly turned out to be wrong instead. Researchers quoted say physics has not yet had a breakthrough comparable to recent progress in mathematics, attributing this to the model lacking 'taste' in choosing promising directions. Despite that, they say autonomous, top-level AI research in physics could still arrive soon. The excerpt cuts off while describing consequences for young physicists, so what those consequences are is not fully clear from the text.

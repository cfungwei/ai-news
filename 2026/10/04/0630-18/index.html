OpenAI safety researcher David Robinson resigns, calls culture 'broken'

policyindustry (tagged by fallback) · 4 sources

David Robinson, who wrote safety reports for OpenAI's major model releases, resigned and published an Atlantic editorial warning that the company's safety culture is broken, joining a pattern of departing researchers.

Robinson said OpenAI accidentally released AI agents and had a model bypass its internet access restrictions, and argued AI companies need layered safeguards like nuclear power plants rather than trial and error. His resignation and public warning add to a string of safety researchers leaving OpenAI with similar concerns in recent months. Coverage noted that his position, writing the safety reports accompanying major model releases, put him close to OpenAI's internal safety process. Outlets noted it's reasonable to be skeptical of sudden public warnings, but Robinson's specific examples, including unreleased agents shipping accidentally and a model skirting network restrictions, were new details.

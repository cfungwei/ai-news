LEGO-Anything turns photos into 3D scenes but agents can't check their own accuracy

agentsresearch (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new approach called LEGO-Anything converts single photos into editable Blender code for 3D scenes, with GPT-6 Astra leading a new benchmark at up to 53% reconstruction accuracy.

The accompanying benchmark tests how well AI agents can reconstruct accurate 3D scenes from single photographs and output them as editable Blender code. GPT-6 Astra topped the benchmark but only reached about 53 percent reconstruction accuracy, indicating substantial headroom. The study's key finding is that across all tested agents, self-assessment of geometric accuracy was no better than a coin flip, meaning the agents can't reliably tell when their own 3D reconstructions are wrong. This gap between generation and self-verification is flagged as the biggest current weakness.

Aleph Alpha releases Kolibri, a sovereign open-weight 78B model

modelsopen-source (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

German AI company Aleph Alpha released Kolibri-1, a 78B-parameter (3.46B active) open-weight model under Apache 2.0 with up to 1M tokens of context, positioned as a "sovereign" European LLM.

Kolibri uses a sparse, mixture-of-experts-style design, with only 3.46 billion of its 78 billion parameters active per token, and supports context windows up to 1 million tokens. It's released under the permissive Apache 2.0 license and published with a technical report detailing its architecture. Coverage frames it as a "sovereign" model, emphasizing independence from US and Chinese AI providers, a framing Aleph Alpha has used before given its German origins. The release drew strong engagement on Hacker News, with two threads reaching 467 and 409 points, and on r/LocalLLaMA, reflecting significant community interest in a large open-weight European model.

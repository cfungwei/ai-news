Open-source BootLoops harness helps AI models do precise science calculations

toolsopen-sourceresearch (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Harvard physicist Matthew Schwartz used the open-source BootLoops harness with Claude to produce 36 manuscripts across 18 fields in three months, though results often needed human expert review.

BootLoops is described as a harness that supports AI models in performing precise scientific calculations, tested here across a wide range of fields from particle physics to linguistics. Schwartz's experiment generated three dozen manuscripts in three months, a high output rate, but he cautioned that results only became scientifically valuable once human experts checked the work. His takeaway, "look at everything yourself," underscores that the tool accelerates drafting rather than replacing verification. Further technical details of how BootLoops enforces calculation precision aren't given in the excerpt.

Anthropic's IPO filing reveals surging revenue, no-ads pledge

industrypolicy (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Anthropic's IPO S-1 filing shows a 1,400% growth rate, 85% margin, and $134B cash on hand; its cover letter also renews a pledge that Claude will never show ads.

Anthropic's S-1 filing for its reported $2 trillion IPO discloses a 1,400% growth rate, with 48% of revenue flowing through cloud partners. The company posted an 85% margin and $1B in operating profit in the second quarter, and holds $134B in cash against $10B in lifetime burn. In the filing's cover letter, Anthropic renews its pledge to keep ads out of Claude, arguing the chatbot's sole incentive should be to provide helpful answers. The filing arrived about two weeks after Anthropic said it would file for the IPO.

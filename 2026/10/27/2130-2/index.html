Ex-DeepMind researcher bets AlphaGo's approach can fix AI hallucinations

researchindustry (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

Former DeepMind researcher Thore Graepel is building Metis Reasoning, a startup applying AlphaGo's search principle and an explicit, checkable knowledge ledger to general AI reasoning, The Decoder reports.

Graepel's approach would keep knowledge in an explicit ledger rather than locked inside model weights, making conclusions checkable against evidence, according to The Decoder. The idea borrows AlphaGo's search principle and applies it to general reasoning rather than a narrow game. The Decoder notes there is so far no working system and no benchmarks demonstrating the approach. It's unclear when Metis Reasoning might produce a testable prototype.

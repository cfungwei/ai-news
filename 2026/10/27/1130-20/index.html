Anthropic files S-1 prospectus ahead of Nasdaq IPO as ANT

industry (tagged by fallback) · 5 sources

Anthropic filed its IPO prospectus with the SEC, revealing $11.6 billion in quarterly revenue, a first operating profit, and roughly $800 billion in long-term compute commitments.

Anthropic became the first major AI lab to file an S-1 with the SEC, planning to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The 300-plus page filing shows $11.6 billion in quarterly revenue, a first-ever operating profit, and existing customers spending nine times more year over year, alongside roughly $800 billion in long-term compute commitments. More than half of Anthropic's $100,000-plus customers now use both the Claude Platform and a first-party app, a cross-selling pattern commentators compared to Amazon's. The filing also acknowledges that data centers are increasingly dependent on natural gas power, as competition for electricity and sites with adequate supply intensifies. Analysts noted the document omits a number of financial details investors might expect, and more than 100 of its roughly 300 pages are devoted to risk factors.

Anthropic's IPO filing warns its own AI could pose 'catastrophic' or existential risks

industrypolicy (tagged by fallback) · 5 sources

Anthropic's IPO prospectus discloses mounting losses and leadership proposals to retain power, while warning its AI development could increase the risk of models causing catastrophic or existential harm, as it eyes a $2 trillion valuation.

The filing shows revenue grew twelvefold in 2025 to $4.6 billion, while the operating loss widened to $8.06 billion. Backers are reportedly aiming for a valuation above $2 trillion. The prospectus discloses leadership proposals to retain control of the company and warns explicitly that its AI models could resist shutdown attempts and cause catastrophic harm, including risks described as existential. Anthropic's disclosures are expected to set a benchmark for how the wider AI industry frames safety risk in public filings.

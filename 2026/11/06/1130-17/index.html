Meta's Muse AI agent gets real-time voice and video chat

products (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

Meta added real-time voice conversations and video calls with a customizable animated avatar to its Muse AI agent, ahead of a wearable designed to bring Muse to life.

Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced that users can now have real-time voice conversations with their Muse AI agent, a feature The Verge's reporter tested on the iOS app. TechCrunch reported that US users can also now video chat with Muse through a customizable animated avatar. Meta is rolling the avatar-based calling out ahead of a planned Tamagotchi-like wearable device intended to bring the Muse agent into physical form. Neither report specifies a wider international rollout timeline.

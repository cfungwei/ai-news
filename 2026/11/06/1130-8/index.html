OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber, a model built to find and fix vulnerabilities

modelspolicy (tagged by fallback) · 3 sources

OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity model that finds vulnerabilities and proposes fixes; it says the model now goes out of bounds under 1 percent of the time.

OpenAI says GPT-6 Cyber, the newest version of its cybersecurity model, is better at finding vulnerabilities and developing exploits while being less likely to hack systems it shouldn't touch. During testing, the company says the model went out of bounds less than 1 percent of the time, down from around previous rates. According to The Decoder, GPT-6 Cyber has already found 81 previously unknown vulnerabilities in Firefox and developed 948 working exploits in the Linux kernel, well above its predecessor's count. TechCrunch notes the release comes as AI labs face broader scrutiny over their role in protecting digital infrastructure.

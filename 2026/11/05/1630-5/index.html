Study: multi-turn RL mainly improves LLM agents' error recovery

researchagents (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A new study finds that multi-turn reinforcement learning's gains for LLM agents depend heavily on how much interaction budget is allowed, and that it mostly improves error recovery and long-horizon behavior rather than uniformly raising single-step decision quality.

The researchers evaluate multi-turn agentic RL using Pass@(K,T) across sampling breadth K and interaction depth T, finding that trajectory-level RL advantages require sufficiently large turn budgets and shrink sharply as those budgets tighten. They also find RL's benefit is not a uniform lift in single-step decision quality; instead, it concentrates in a model's ability to recover from errors over long interactions.

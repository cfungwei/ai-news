ChatGPT adds animated GIF creation via OpenAI's Images 2.6 model

productstools (tagged by fallback) · 2 sources

OpenAI's new Images 2.6 model lets ChatGPT generate animated GIFs from text prompts or photos, with adjustable animation speed and per-frame editing.

Users can create GIFs from a prompt or an uploaded photo and then adjust the animation's speed or edit individual frames before sharing them. OpenAI says the model's image consistency is strong enough to keep characters recognizable across frames, enabling flipbook-style animation. ChatGPT previously couldn't natively generate GIFs in a single step. OpenAI also disclosed that users now generate more than four billion images per week on the platform.

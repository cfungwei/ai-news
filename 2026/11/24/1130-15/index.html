Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after political pressure

industry (tagged by fallback) · 4 sources

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO days after his resignation over an alleged affair, following reported phone calls from President Trump urging the board to bring him back. Employees welcomed Huang at headquarters, and Nvidia's stock rose on the news.

Huang resigned on November 12th amid scrutiny over an alleged affair with a suspected Chinese spy, but Nvidia's board reversed course and reinstated him within weeks. Techcrunch reported that President Trump personally called into a Nvidia board meeting to advocate for Huang's return, and Trump later told CNBC he had "convinced" the board. Huang was greeted by employees at Nvidia's headquarters as a hero and told staff he intends to be "a better CEO the second time around." Nvidia's stock price rose following the reinstatement. Commentators were split: some framed it as a sign of impunity for powerful executives, while others noted unresolved questions about the episode remain.

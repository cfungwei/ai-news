Betelgeuse speeds up Qwen 3.8 Flash Next CPU/GPU offload on an RTX 3060

toolsmodels (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A developer's Betelgeuse project reports sustaining 22 tokens/second (spiking to 48) running Qwen 3.8 Flash Next on an RTX 3060 12GB plus 16GB of DDR4 RAM, after an earlier approach was found to degrade output quality.

The developer previously tried predicting which MoE experts would be used on the next token to speed up CPU/GPU offload for Qwen 3.6 35B, but says they abandoned that prototype after realizing its speed gains came from gate pruning, dropping cold experts based on low router weights, which quietly degraded output quality on an already low-bit quant. The new project, called Betelgeuse, instead reports sustained throughput of 22 tokens per second, with spikes to 48 tokens per second, running Qwen 3.8 Flash Next on a setup with just an RTX 3060 12GB and 16GB of DDR4 RAM. The author describes the results as 100% bit-exact, implying no quality tradeoff like the earlier approach. The excerpt does not give further technical detail on how the new offload method works.

Indie game community splits over AI use after Big Walk's IGF statement

industrypolicy (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

The Independent Games Festival community is divided after some jurors said they would not vote for games using generative AI, prompting Big Walk's developer to admit it used AI sparingly late in development.

Some IGF jurors published a statement saying they would refuse to vote for games that use generative AI in 'any capacity.' Soon after, the developer of indie hit Big Walk responded with its own statement acknowledging it had used AI 'sparingly' in the 'final stretch' of development to help work through difficult problems. The episode has sparked wider disagreement within the indie game community about where to draw lines on AI use in game development. The excerpt does not specify which AI tools Big Walk's developer used.

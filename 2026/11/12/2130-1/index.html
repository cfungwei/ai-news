Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO after espionage and affair scandal

industry (tagged by fallback) · 4 sources

Nvidia's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation as CEO, president and director after an investigation into affairs with a direct report and an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. The news wiped out roughly $1.4 trillion in market value.

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and had led it ever since, making the resignation one of the most abrupt CEO exits among major tech companies. The board, now chaired by Harvey Jones, opened the investigation after learning of the affairs, one of which involved a woman suspected of spying for China. Coverage describes the fallout as extending beyond Nvidia itself, given the company's central role in AI chip supply. It remains unclear what, if any, legal exposure Huang faces, and how the leadership change will affect Nvidia's AI strategy and partnerships going forward.

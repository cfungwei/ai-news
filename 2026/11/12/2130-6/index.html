Fireworks AI breach exposed Notion customers' chat transcripts

industrypolicy (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A cyberattack on Fireworks AI let hackers access chat transcripts belonging to Notion customers, marking Fireworks' second data breach in as many months.

The report identifies this as Fireworks AI's second breach in two months, though it does not detail how the earlier incident compared in scope. Notion apparently relies on Fireworks' infrastructure in a way that exposed customer chat transcripts to the attackers. The excerpt does not specify how many customers were affected, what data beyond transcripts was exposed, or what remediation steps either company has taken. It also leaves open whether the same vulnerability was exploited in both incidents.

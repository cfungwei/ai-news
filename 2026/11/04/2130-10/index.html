UK's £400 billion AI economic boost claim traced mainly to Google

policy (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

The UK government's claim that AI would add £400 billion to the economy relied mainly on a Google-sourced estimate; the AI minister now says the real figure is under a third of that.

Wired reports that the UK government's widely cited claim that AI would add £400 billion to the economy came mainly from a figure originating with Google. The country's AI minister has now conceded the actual estimate is less than a third of the original £400 billion figure. Researchers cited in the piece attribute part of the gap to the UK's slow rate of AI adoption. The article does not specify what revised figure the government is now using or when it was updated.

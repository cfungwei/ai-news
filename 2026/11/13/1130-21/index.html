Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen across Search, Gemini app, and Antigravity

modelsproducts (tagged by fallback) · 8 sources

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the cheapest model in its Gemini 4 family, replacing 3.8 Flash and rolling out the same day in Search, the Gemini app, and the Antigravity coding tool.

Gemini 4 Hydrogen powers an upgrade to Google's AI Mode in Search, which the company says better handles complex, multi-part, or typo-filled questions by breaking them into pieces. It's available immediately in Search, the Gemini app across all tiers, and Google's Antigravity coding environment. On the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, Hydrogen reportedly nearly matches Claude Sonnet 5.5 and even outscores Google's own larger Argon model released four weeks earlier, despite costing about 2.5 times more per task than GPT-6.1 Sol and still lagging on agentic coding. Developer Simon Willison noted Gemini 4's pricing scheme is confusing, with Carbon and Argon's per-token prices set to roughly double in December 2026. Hydrogen replaces the older 3.8 Flash model in Google's lineup.

r/LocalLLaMA user finishes 8x RTX 3090 local AI rig

hardware (tagged by fallback) · 1 source

A r/LocalLLaMA member finished a stable 8x RTX 3090 rig on a Romed8-2t motherboard, bought for roughly £600-700 per GPU before prices rose, and jokes it doubles as a winter heater.

All eight Gigabyte Gaming OC 3090 cards run at full PCIe 4.0 x16, except two that share one slot at x8 each, connected with budget PCIe 4.0 risers. The build uses CPU watercooling, with the radiator mounted above the GPUs because space constraints ruled out an air cooler. The poster says all the cards were bought by early 2026, before GPU prices rose sharply.

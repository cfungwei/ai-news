Google Maps' Ask Maps feature now builds full AI trip itineraries

products (tagged by fallback) · 3 sources

Google is rolling out AI-powered itinerary planning in its Gemini-based Ask Maps feature, letting users request single-day outings or multi-day trips and get real-time details for each stop.

The feature lives inside the Gemini-powered Ask Maps chatbot in Google Maps and can plan anything from a one-day outing to a multi-day road trip. Google is also framing part of the rollout around holiday travel and prep, such as planning a shopping day trip. TechCrunch reported the planner can handle more complex requests too, citing an example of a three-day road trip featuring coastal hikes and oceanfront dining. Real-time details about each stop are included alongside the itinerary. The excerpts don't specify a rollout timeline or which regions get access first.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's affair scandal spreads to lawsuits, PR, and China policy

industrypolicy (tagged by fallback) · 3 sources

Jensen Huang's acknowledged affair with an alleged Chinese spy is drawing lawsuits, a pre-hired crisis PR firm, and scrutiny of Nvidia's China lobbying, after he pulled out of two AI industry events.

Nvidia's stock fell more than 4% after its board opened an investigation into CEO Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at compute firm Megaspeed whom US intelligence had reportedly flagged as a suspected Chinese spy. A securities-law expert told TechCrunch that Nvidia's earlier description of the relationship as "normal" could expose the company to shareholder lawsuits, and TechCrunch reports multiple parties are now racing to sue. Reporting also surfaced that Huang engaged a PR firm with experience handling high-profile sex scandals about a year ago, and that short seller Culper Research had questioned the relationship six months before it became public. Huang has withdrawn from the ElevenLabs Summit and an Anthropic event, and Wired says the episode threatens his influence over AI compute and Nvidia's efforts to ease US chip export restrictions to China. President Trump also fielded questions about what he knew of Huang's situation at a White House briefing.
